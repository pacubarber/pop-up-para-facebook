    FAQ

    What do I need to shoot?

    Whatever you already shoot on your phone works. If you don't know where to start, we send you a script with 3 or 4 shots: what to say, where to look and how long it should run.

    How fast do I get it back?

    48 to 72 hours per video, counted from when we have all your footage. For a 6-video bundle we agree on a delivery schedule.

    What if I don't like it?

    Every video includes 2 rounds of revisions. Send all your changes in one list and we fix them. Want to try us first? Start with a single reel.

    Is the music copyright-safe?

    We only use licensed or original music, so your videos don't get muted or blocked.

    How do I pay?

    Up front, in US dollars, by PayPal, Wise or bank transfer. We start editing once the payment is confirmed.

    Who owns the videos?

    You do. We only ask permission to show some of them in our portfolio.
